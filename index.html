The "Break to Learn" Philosophy

Theoretical knowledge often crumbles in production. To build robust software, you must understand the edge cases, the bottlenecks, and the vulnerabilities. Whether it's finding a race condition in an OS kernel or analyzing a supply chain attack, our curriculum bridges the gap between academic theory and rigorous engineering practice.
